Should you start Jordyn Tyson in Week 11?

Yes, Jordyn Tyson is a strong start consideration for your lineup this week. His recent target volume makes him a reliable WR option to fill your flex or WR2 spot.

The rise of Jordyn Tyson

Jordyn Tyson has grabbed the attention of fantasy managers following an immediate impact in the New Orleans offense. At 22 years old and in his rookie season, the receiver has averaged 12 PPR pts across 2 appearances. After a quiet Week 9 with 5 pts, Tyson exploded in Week 10 against Carolina, recording 19 PPR pts on 11 catches and 13 targets for 80 yards. This massive surge in participation positions him as a relevant asset. The Saints clearly trust his ability to generate yards after the catch, cementing him as a key piece added in 228008 leagues recently, reflecting the widespread excitement regarding his immediate potential and role within the team's passing scheme.

Analyzing the Chicago matchup

For Week 11, Tyson faces an intriguing challenge on the road against Chicago. Our FX model projects 12.6 PPR pts, 9.9 Half PPR, and 7.2 STD, placing him as the WR17 for the week. The MATCHUP NEUTRAL is favorable, considering the Bears defense allows 29.7 pts per game to wide receivers, ranking as the 21st defense in the league. While it is not the easiest matchup, Tyson's trend of receiving high target volume offsets any defensive risk. His ability to remain active and productive after overcoming his recent injury proves that his ceiling is legitimate, making him a reliable player for the current offensive game plans in New Orleans.

Fantasy lineup strategy

Consistency in target volume is the most important metric for emerging receivers. Tyson has shown he can be the primary focus of the aerial attack when called upon. Facing a defense that allows average production, his WR17 projection suggests he has a safe floor for this matchup. If you need to fill a flex spot due to bye weeks or injuries, Tyson is the most logical choice. Do not wait for other managers in your league to claim him; his upward trajectory suggests his value will continue to rise as he adapts to the professional game speed and builds better chemistry with his quarterback throughout the remainder of the season.
