Should you start Tyler Higbee in Week 8?

Tyler Higbee is a volatile option for Week 8 lineups. His recent target share has dipped following a solid mid-season stretch, but a favorable matchup against the Chargers keeps him on the fantasy radar.

Recent performance and current season context

At 33 years old and with ten years of NFL experience, Tyler Higbee is experiencing an up-and-down campaign with the Rams. Over seven games, he has accumulated 54 total PPR points, averaging 7.7 per contest. After strong outings in Weeks 4 and 5 with 11.7 and 13.3 fantasy points, his production plummeted in his last two games against Arizona and Las Vegas, scoring just 4.2 and 3.4 points respectively, hampered by fewer red-zone targets and overall offensive struggles.

Furthermore, severe injuries hitting the Rams wide receiver corps could force the offense to rely more heavily on Higbee over the middle, potentially boosting his volume.
