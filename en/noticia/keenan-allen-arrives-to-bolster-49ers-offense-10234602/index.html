Keenan Allen arrives to bolster 49ers offense

  • San Francisco acquires veteran Keenan Allen from Colts
  • Allen fills critical void in injury plagued receiver room
  • Boosts PPR fantasy floor into WR3 or Flex range

What happened

The 49ers finalized a trade before the deadline to bring in veteran wide receiver Keenan Allen from the Colts. San Francisco acted decisively to address severe depth issues caused by injuries across their receiving corps. Allen arrives to provide immediate reliability on intermediate routes within an efficient offensive structure.

Fantasy impact

Allen receives a noticeable fantasy boost, especially in PPR scoring and 10 or 12 team leagues. Moving into an offense that needs target earners, he steps into a path for consistent targets. View Allen as a strong WR3 or Flex start capable of demanding 6 to 8 targets per game.

What changes for the team

Allen instantly steps in as San Francisco's primary possession receiver on key 3rd downs. His veteran presence stabilizes the passing attack and helps sustain long drives for the entire offense.

What to do: Target Keenan Allen in trades or start him as a solid PPR Flex immediately.
