49ers release wide receiver Brandon Aiyuk in Week 11

  • San Francisco released Brandon Aiyuk as roster exemption ended.
  • Aiyuk is recovering from severe ACL and MCL knee injury.
  • Deebo Samuel and George Kittle main targets moving forward.

What happened

The San Francisco 49ers announced the release of wide receiver Brandon Aiyuk on Monday, November 11, as his roster exemption period came to an end. Aiyuk remains sidelined recovering from a major knee injury involving his ACL and MCL. General manager John Lynch made the roster move while the receiver continues his lengthy rehabilitation process.

Fantasy impact

In redraft fantasy formats, Brandon Aiyuk must be dropped immediately across 100% of leagues. His departure from the 49ers solidifies Deebo Samuel and George Kittle as the top 2 passing options in this offense. Jauan Jennings gains fantasy value as the team's WR2, making him a viable flex target in 12 team PPR leagues.

What changes for the team

The 49ers offense clears roster uncertainty and focuses its target tree on George Kittle and Deebo Samuel. Jauan Jennings steps into a primary WR2 role for Week 11.

What to do: Drop Brandon Aiyuk from your fantasy roster immediately if he was still on your bench.
