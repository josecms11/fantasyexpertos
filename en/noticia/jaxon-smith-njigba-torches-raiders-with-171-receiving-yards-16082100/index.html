Jaxon Smith-Njigba torches Raiders with 171 receiving yards

  • NFL leading receiver caught 8 passes for 171 yards.
  • Exploded for 144 receiving yards in 3rd quarter alone.
  • Projected for 22.7 fantasy points in FX Week 10 model.

What happened

Jaxon Smith-Njigba cemented his status as the NFL leading receiver by propelling Seattle to a 21-13 comeback victory against the Raiders. The standout wideout reeled in 8 catches for 171 yards, with an astonishing 144 yards coming in the 3rd quarter alone. This dominant surge erased a 3-point halftime deficit, proving he is the undisputed engine driving this Seahawks aerial attack every single week.

Fantasy impact

Smith-Njigba is locked in as an elite WR1 across all PPR and standard formats. The FX model projects him for 22.7 pts in Week 10, ranking him as the top wide receiver overall. His massive target share and big-play ability give him an elite weekly ceiling, making him an automatic every-week starter that you simply cannot bench under any circumstance.

What changes for the team

Seattle passing offense completely revolves around Smith-Njigba. Defenses shifting coverage his way frees up underneath space for complimentary playmakers and lightens defensive fronts for the running game.
