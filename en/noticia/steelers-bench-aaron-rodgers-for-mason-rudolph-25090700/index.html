Steelers bench Aaron Rodgers for Mason Rudolph

  • Mike McCarthy benched Aaron Rodgers ahead of Week 12.
  • Mason Rudolph takes over as starting QB against Broncos.
  • Pittsburgh dropped 2 straight games after starting 6-2.

What happened

The Steelers made a major quarterback change ahead of Week 12. Head coach Mike McCarthy decided to bench veteran passer Aaron Rodgers following 2 consecutive losses. Pittsburgh opened the campaign with a strong 6-2 record, but recent offensive struggles forced the coaching staff to name backup quarterback Mason Rudolph as the starter for their upcoming matchup against the Broncos.

Fantasy impact

Aaron Rodgers loses all fantasy appeal while sitting on the bench, making him an easy drop in standard 1 QB leagues. Mason Rudolph becomes a emergency waiver option strictly for Superflex and 2 QB setups. This decision lowers the overall passing upside of the offense, bringing heavy volatility to all Pittsburgh pass catchers heading into Week 12 fantasy lineups.

What changes for the team

The offensive scheme may shift toward a heavier ground attack to protect Mason Rudolph. Pittsburgh receivers face a lower floor and reduced efficiency until the new starter establishes rhythm in the passing game.

What to do: Bench Aaron Rodgers in all formats and look for QB streamer options on waivers.

Players: Aaron Rodgers, Mason Rudolph
