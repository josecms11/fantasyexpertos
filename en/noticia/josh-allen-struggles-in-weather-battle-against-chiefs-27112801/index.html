Josh Allen Struggles in Weather Battle Against Chiefs

  • Josh Allen passes for just 81 yards in victory.
  • Bills top Chiefs 13-10 on last second field goal.
  • Severe weather conditions restricted overall passing production.

What happened

In a matchup dominated by severe weather, Josh Allen endured one of his quietest passing games of the year, finishing with just 81 yards through the air. Despite the passing game coming to a near halt, Buffalo managed to edge Kansas City 13-10 thanks to a dramatic 57-yard game-winning field goal by Tyler Bass as time expired in week 12.

Fantasy impact

While Josh Allen produced a disappointing fantasy stat line with 81 passing yards, fantasy managers should not overreact. The abysmal weather conditions were the primary cause for the low-scoring contest. Allen remains an elite QB1 option with a projected baseline of 20.6 PPR points. Keep starting him in all formats without hesitation heading into week 12.

What changes for the team

Buffalo's passing attack experienced a temporary freeze due to bad weather. Wide receivers saw reduced floor values, while kicker Tyler Bass provided the deciding points with a clutch 57-yard boot.
