Jaylen Warren explodes for 143 rushing yards

  • Tied career high with 143 rushing yards.
  • Celebrated 28th birthday as team's offensive engine.
  • Solidified status as elite fantasy running back.

What happened

Jaylen Warren celebrated his 28th birthday by putting up a memorable performance with 143 rushing yards. This total tied his career high and cemented his status as the primary engine of Pittsburgh's offense. The running back has shown remarkable growth throughout the season, consistently earning yards after contact and dominating the backfield touches within the team's offensive scheme.

Fantasy impact

Jaylen Warren's production firmly establishes him as an undeniable RB1 in both PPR and standard formats moving forward. His heavy workload provides a safe floor of 15 points per week. If Warren is on your roster, do not overthink it; he is a locked-in weekly starter. Fantasy managers looking to trade for him will face a steep price after this 143-yard rushing performance.

What changes for the team

Warren's rise significantly reduces touch opportunities for the rest of Pittsburgh's backfield. The offense relies heavily on the ground game to establish tempo, relieving pressure on the quarterback while keeping the defense rested on the sideline.

What to do: Start Jaylen Warren as your locked-in RB1 in all fantasy leagues.
