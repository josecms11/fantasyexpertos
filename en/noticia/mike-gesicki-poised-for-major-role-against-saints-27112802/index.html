Mike Gesicki Poised for Major Role Against Saints

  • Mike Gesicki expected to be key factor for Bengals.
  • Cincinnati targets Saints defensive weaknesses over the middle.
  • Solid waiver wire target for deep PPR formats.

What happened

The Cincinnati Bengals view tight end Mike Gesicki as a pivotal weapon in their offensive game plan for the upcoming week 12 clash against the New Orleans Saints. Gesicki's ability to soft-spot coverage zones down the seam offers quarterback Joe Burrow a reliable intermediate target to sustain drives and exploit matchups in the middle of the field.

Fantasy impact

Mike Gesicki rises as a sneaky waiver wire streaming candidate at tight end for week 12. In 12-team PPR leagues, his projected target share gives him a dependable TE2 floor. If he converts a red-zone target into 1 TD, he can easily deliver TE1 value for managers dealing with injuries or bye weeks.

What changes for the team

Gesicki's increased presence over the middle draws defensive attention away from Ja'Marr Chase, giving Joe Burrow a trusted safety valve on crucial 3rd-down conversions and red-zone situations throughout the game.

What to do: Pick up Mike Gesicki off waivers if you need a viable TE option in week 12.

Players: Mike Gesicki, Ja'Marr Chase, Joe Burrow
