Josh Downs traded to Chiefs at NFL trade deadline

  • Josh Downs joins Kansas City via official trade deal
  • Kansas City bolsters receiver corps ahead of Week 10
  • Patrick Mahomes gains new weapon in the passing game

What happened

On the NFL trade deadline day, the Kansas City Chiefs completed a trade to acquire receiver Josh Downs. The move came during a busy day across the league that featured 6 trades in the final hours of the deadline window. Kansas City aims to provide additional options for their passing attack starting in Week 10.

Fantasy impact

Downs sees a clear boost in fantasy football value by joining Kansas City's system. In PPR and Half-PPR formats, he becomes a solid FLEX or WR3 option with upside as he learns the playbook. If he is available on your league's waiver wire, he is worth targeting immediately to bolster your wide receiver depth.

What changes for the team

Adding Downs provides Kansas City's receiving corps with a quick threat on short and intermediate routes. This creates spacing for tight ends and relieves pressure on the remaining offensive weapons heading into the second half of the season.

What to do: Add Josh Downs in PPR leagues if available on waivers.
