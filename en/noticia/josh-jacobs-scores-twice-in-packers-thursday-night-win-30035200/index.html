Josh Jacobs Scores Twice in Packers Thursday Night Win

  • Jacobs scores 2 TD in Green Bay's 27-19 victory.
  • Green Bay improves to 5-3 after beating Carolina.
  • Jacobs cements his status as an elite every-week RB1.

What happened

Josh Jacobs powered Green Bay's offense by scoring 2 TD in a 27-19 victory over the Panthers to kick off week 8. Carolina made a strong push in the fourth quarter to tighten the game, but the Packers held on to improve their record to 5-3 on Thursday night. Jacobs once again proved to be the focal point of the red-zone attack.

Fantasy impact

With a 2 TD performance, Josh Jacobs solidifies his standing as an elite, matchup-proof RB1 in all fantasy formats, including standard and PPR leagues. Having Kaleb Johnson out by coach decision left the backfield completely centered around Jacobs near the goal line. His consistent touchdown upside and heavy workload give fantasy managers a reliable, high-end floor every single week.

What changes for the team

Jacobs provides Green Bay with crucial offensive balance, relieving pressure from Jordan Love. His red-zone effectiveness keeps drives alive and allows the Packers to control game script, strengthening the entire unit as the team moves to 5-3 on the season.

What to do: Keep Josh Jacobs locked into your fantasy starting lineup as an elite RB1.
