Chase Brown Raises Starting Concerns in Fantasy Leagues

  • Analysts evaluate Chase Brown's status as a fantasy starter.
  • His weekly touch volume shows recent fluctuations.
  • His lineup value requires careful assessment for Week 8.

What happened

Chase Brown's production comes under review by ESPN analysts heading into Week 8. Following games with inconsistent performance, debate surrounds whether he maintains RB2 value in fantasy lineups. Cincinnati's coaching staff continues to adjust offensive schemes, directly impacting touch distribution and ground efficiency for Brown on the field.

Fantasy impact

For fantasy managers in 10 or 12 team PPR leagues, Chase Brown shifts from a reliable RB2 to a volatile option. For Week 8, it is safer to treat him as a touchdown-dependent RB3 or FLEX. If you possess backfield options with guaranteed volume, consider benching Brown until his touch floor stabilizes.

What changes for the team

Cincinnati's offense is diversifying ground touches, reducing reliance on a single back. This committee approach demands higher efficiency per attempt, introducing unpredictability to the unit's fantasy production overall.

What to do: Explore bench alternatives before committing Chase Brown to your Week 8 lineup.
