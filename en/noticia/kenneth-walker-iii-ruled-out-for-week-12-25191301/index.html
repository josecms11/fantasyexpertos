Kenneth Walker III ruled out for Week 12

  • Kenneth Walker III ruled out against the Bills.
  • Groin injury sidelines the Chiefs running back.

What happened

The Chiefs officially ruled out running back Kenneth Walker III for Thursday's Week 12 showdown against the Bills. Walker III suffered a groin injury during practice that prevents him from taking the field. The team confirmed his absence on Wednesday, leaving Kansas City without their main back right before the Thanksgiving games kick off.

Fantasy impact

With Kenneth Walker III officially ruled out with a groin injury, he cannot be started in any PPR, standard, or Half-PPR fantasy formats for Week 12. Move him directly to your bench or IR slot right now to free up an active roster spot for a waiver replacement before Thursday.

What changes for the team

Kenneth Walker III's absence reshuffles the workload in the Chiefs backfield. Kansas City backup running backs will need to step up and handle touches against the Bills defense in Week 12.

What to do: Bench Kenneth Walker III and grab a replacement off waivers.
