Odell Beckham Jr. Added To Vikings Active Roster

  • Odell Beckham Jr. signed to Vikings active roster
  • Available for Monday night showdown against Buffalo Bills
  • Speculative bench stash strictly in very deep leagues

What happened

Minnesota officially signed veteran wide receiver Odell Beckham Jr. to their 53-man roster, clearing the way for his team debut on Monday night against the Buffalo Bills. With Jauan Jennings ruled out due to a calf injury and several offensive contributors sidelined, the Vikings added Beckham to provide depth and reliable hands for newly installed starting quarterback Carson Wentz in a high-stakes matchup.

Fantasy impact

Do not start Odell Beckham Jr. in Week 9 lineups; his snap share will likely be capped in his first game action. However, he represents an intriguing speculative waiver stash in 14-team or dynasty formats. If Beckham quickly builds chemistry with Carson Wentz and establishes himself in 3-receiver sets, he could provide depth scoring during the upcoming fantasy playoff push as a rotational possession target.

What changes for the team

Beckham provides veteran experience and route precision on third downs for quarterback Carson Wentz, stabilizing a depleted wide receiver room without disrupting the core offensive identity against Buffalo.

What to do: Stash Odell Beckham Jr. on your bench only in very deep leagues while monitoring his snap share Monday night.
