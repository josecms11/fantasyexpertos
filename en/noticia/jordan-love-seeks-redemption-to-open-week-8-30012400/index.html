Jordan Love seeks redemption to open Week 8

  • Green Bay hosts Carolina on Thursday Night Football.
  • Both franchises enter looking to bounce back from Week 7 losses.
  • Jordan Love aims to regain form in front of home fans.

What happened

The Green Bay Packers host the Carolina Panthers at Lambeau Field to kick off Week 8 on Thursday Night Football. Both quarterbacks, Jordan Love and Bryce Young, enter this matchup desperate to recover momentum after dropping their Week 7 games. Playing at home gives Green Bay an ideal platform to iron out offensive mistakes and get back into the win column.

Fantasy impact

In 12-team and superflex formats, Jordan Love remains a viable QB2 option with QB1 ceiling if he capitalizes on the home matchup. Meanwhile, Bryce Young is a high-risk play strictly for deep leagues or 2-QB setups. If you need early points to start Week 8, Green Bay's passing attack offers a much safer floor for fantasy managers.

What changes for the team

Green Bay's offense relies on Jordan Love establishing early tempo to keep receiver targets engaged. Carolina needs clean execution from Bryce Young to keep this road contest competitive throughout.

What to do: Start Jordan Love as a solid QB2 option to kick off Week 8.

Players: Jordan Love, Bryce Young
