Jonah Coleman shines with 111 yards and 3 TDs

  • Ran for 111 yards and 3 TDs against Las Vegas.

What happened

Rookie running back Jonah Coleman enjoyed his best game of the season, rushing for 111 yards and 3 TDs in Denver's 38-24 victory over the Raiders. On an emotional Sunday honoring Tom Jackson, Coleman spearheaded the ground attack and capitalized on scoring drives to secure the win for the Broncos.

Fantasy impact

Coleman demonstrated his high fantasy ceiling across PPR and standard formats. By scoring 3 TDs and topping 100 yards, he solidifies his role as a rising RB2 or Flex starter for Week 11. His goal-line usage makes him a valuable asset if you need running back depth for your starting lineup.

What changes for the team

Coleman's breakout balances Denver's offense and takes pressure off the passing game. While the backfield retains rotation, Coleman takes control of high-value touch opportunities inside the team's red zone.

What to do: Pick him up off waivers immediately or plug him into your RB2 spot.
