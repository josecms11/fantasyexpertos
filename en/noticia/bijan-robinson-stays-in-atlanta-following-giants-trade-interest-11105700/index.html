Bijan Robinson stays in Atlanta following Giants trade interest

  • Giants inquired about the star running back before the trade deadline.
  • Atlanta's steep asking price completely shut down any potential deal.
  • Remains an elite RB1 projected for 20.2 points in Week 10.

What happened

According to reports from ESPN, the New York Giants explored trade possibilities for Bijan Robinson prior to the trade deadline. However, Atlanta's extremely steep asking price prevented any move from happening. New York sought a top-tier running back to bolster its roster, but the Falcons refused to part with their superstar, ensuring Robinson stays firmly established as the central pillar of Atlanta's offensive backfield for the remainder of the season.

Fantasy impact

Staying in Atlanta secures Bijan Robinson as an undisputed top-tier RB1 across all fantasy formats, especially in PPR and 10 or 12-team leagues. Projected for 20.2 points in Week 10, he avoids the disruption of learning a completely new offensive scheme midseason. Fantasy managers retain his massive rushing volume and passing-down usage without committee concerns, guaranteeing a reliable weekly floor of at least 15 touches alongside an elite ceiling.

What changes for the team

Atlanta reasserts its complete commitment to Bijan Robinson as the centerpiece of its ground attack. Meanwhile, the New York Giants fail to upgrade their backfield after trade talks collapsed, leaving their offensive unit forced to rely solely on their current depth chart heading into the second half of the season.

What to do: Keep Bijan Robinson locked in as an unbenchable starter in your Week 10 lineups.
